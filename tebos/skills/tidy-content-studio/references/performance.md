# Reading performance honestly

## What to look at, by the post's job

| Job | Primary signal | Supporting |
|---|---|---|
| Reach (new people) | Reach from non-followers, shares | Plays, follows |
| Trust | Saves, comments with substance, average watch time | Profile visits |
| Enquiry | Link taps, DMs asking a real question, enquiries logged | Profile visits |

## How sure can we be

- **Fewer than 10 posts of a kind:** describe; don't conclude. Say "seen once" or "seen twice".
- **Compare like with like:** same format, similar posting time, a similar week. Note anything that differs (a
  holiday, a paid boost, a collaborator's share).
- **A pattern** needs 3 or more posts showing it, and it is still a hypothesis to test next week.
- **Never report a rate without its base:** "4 saves out of 310 reached", not "1.3% save rate" alone.
- **No vanity verdicts.** "Viral" isn't a result; enquiries, bookings and sales are. When those aren't tracked, say
  so.

## The review (job 6), in this order

1. What was posted (from the export).
2. The top 3 and bottom 3 by each post's own job, with numbers and bases.
3. What differs between them that we can see (hook, length, format, topic, time).
4. What we can't tell from this data.
5. One test for next week: change one thing.
