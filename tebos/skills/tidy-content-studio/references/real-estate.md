# Property listings and the founder as an agent

The founder is a candidate property practitioner at Pelser Real Estate. Agency rules come before content ideas.

## Before anything is posted

- **The principal approves** every listing advert and anything that speaks for the agency.
- **The agency's name** appears on every listing post. The founder's status is stated as the agency and the PPRA
  require; confirm the exact wording with the principal and keep it in this file once given: `[to confirm: wording]`.
- **The seller's consent** is needed for the photos, the video and the address detail shown. Never show a street
  number, a gate code, the interior of a lived-in home with personal items, or a security weakness without the
  seller's agreement.
- **No buyer or seller details** (names, numbers, finance) in any post, comment or caption.

## Only stated facts

- Use only what the listing record states: price, bedrooms, bathrooms, parking, size, suburb, highlights.
- **Missing facts:** `[to confirm: …]`. Never "spacious" for an unmeasured room, never "walking distance" without a
  measured distance, never an estimated rental yield.
- **No misleading words.** No "investment opportunity of the year", "guaranteed growth", "below market value" (unless
  the principal approves, with evidence), or "won't last".

## The listing content pack (job 9)

1. **Shot list.** Exterior hero at golden hour; entrance; living areas; kitchen; main bedroom; bathroom; outdoor area;
   the view or the street feel; 3 vertical clips of 5–8 seconds for the reel.
2. **Reel (20–30 seconds):**
   - hook: the single strongest true feature, on screen in the first 2 seconds;
   - 4 cuts;
   - price and suburb card;
   - "Enquire with Pelser" call to action.
3. **Carousel (6–8 slides):** cover, features (stated only), floor area, location card (suburb only), and a last
   slide with the enquiry route.
4. **Caption:** a first line with the strongest true feature; the stated facts as a short list; the agency line; up to
   5 hashtags (suburb, city, property type).
5. **Portal text:** about 120 words of facts first, then feel; the agency's required details at the end.

## Lead handling from posts

- Comments and DMs that ask about a listing are **drafted** replies only. The reply moves the person to the agency's
  own channel, with no price negotiation in public.
- Every enquiry goes into the agency's pipeline under the principal's rules. In TEPOS it is recorded as a reference
  (B-001), never with personal details.
