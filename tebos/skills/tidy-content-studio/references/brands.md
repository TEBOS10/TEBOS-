# The brands

## TEBOS {#tebos}

- **What it is:** business operating systems for small and growing businesses, starting at R500 a month (Small),
  R2,500 (Medium), R7,500 (Company). Monthly, with a 6-month minimum.
- **Voice:** plain and confident. Show the system: before and after, the board, the flow. No hype words ("revolutionise",
  "game-changer", "10x"). No AI-novelty selling: sell the outcome (fewer things falling through, faster replies,
  numbers you can see).
- **Pillars:**
  1. What breaks in a growing business (the founder as the bottleneck, the missed follow-up);
  2. How a system fixes it (one flow, shown);
  3. Building TEBOS in public (honest progress, including what didn't work);
  4. An invitation (the free self-check, a demo).
- **Never:** client names or screenshots without consent; invented results; calling a concept build a client.

## BAME {#bame}

- **What it is:** brand management for sport: athletes, clubs, academies and events (bamemarketing.site).
- **Voice:** energetic, proud, South African, commercial. Sport is entertainment and business.
- **Pillars:**
  1. The athlete's or club's story;
  2. Behind the scenes of the work;
  3. Commercial know-how (sponsorship readiness, what partners look for);
  4. Results, only with the client's consent.
- **Never:** guaranteed sponsorship, coverage or deals; athletes (especially minors) shown or named without consent
  (a guardian's for minors); club logos used without permission.

## Property Revenue Architecture {#pra}

- **What it is:** helping property owners understand underperforming property, organise the evidence, compare routes,
  and coordinate qualified specialists. It is not an agency, lender, lawyer or valuer.
- **Voice:** calm, expert, evidence-first.
- **Pillars:**
  1. Questions owners should ask;
  2. How to read a property's numbers;
  3. Case lessons (anonymised, with consent);
  4. What a specialist does and when to call one.
- **Never:** valuations, legal, tax or lending advice; promised returns. Keep PRA separate from the Pelser listings
  unless the principal approves the wording (the property regulator warns about wording that confuses which firm acts).
