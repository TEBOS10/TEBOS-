## What that means for the page

- **Enquiries and sales** first: getting customers (access to markets) is the most-named need.
- **Money in and out** next: cash flow and cost control decide whether a small business survives.
- **Taking work off the founder** and **knowing the numbers**: efficiency and operational reporting are what
  growing and mid-sized businesses buy automation for.
- **Websites**: still the single largest line of small-business technology spend.
- **Security and approvals**: stability and trust, and the reason TEBOS keeps a person on every money or legal decision.

Small businesses buy the first two rows; mid-sized and larger ones buy reporting, connected systems and approvals.
Every card on the page says whether TEBOS runs it today, runs it once a tool is connected, designs it for the
business's own team, or hasn't built it yet.
