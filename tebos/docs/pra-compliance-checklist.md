## The route without a matric (checked 20 Oct on theppra.org.za)

**The 12 November PDE isn't open to you yet.** The PPRA's "Eligibility for PDE" page says every exam candidate must
already hold a valid FFC. A candidate practitioner must also have completed the NQF 4 Real Estate qualification (or an
exemption from it) and six months of practical work. So there is nothing to enrol for by 30 October.

| Route | Needs a matric? | What it involves |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Candidate route (open now)** | **Not listed by the PPRA.** Its page names a 12-month candidacy under a principal or a full-status practitioner, a logbook, the FETC: Real Estate (NQF 4) and then PDE 4. Training providers set their own entry rules: some ask for Grade 11 or a matric, and recognition of prior learning (RPL) can count experience | 1. The agency applies for your candidate FFC. 2. You enrol with an accredited NQF 4 provider that accepts your schooling, or through RPL. 3. Logbook and practical work. 4. PDE 4 once you're eligible |
| B. Competency-based pathway (from 1 Aug 2026) | **Yes, a National Senior Certificate** | Closed to you until you have a matric |
| C. Adult matric (Amended Senior Certificate), alongside A | It is the matric | For adults aged 21 and over who have Grade 9. The Department of Basic Education reports that registration for the May/June 2027 exams closes on **5 February 2027**. This later opens route B and widens your options |

**What to do:**
1. Ask your principal whether the agency will register you as a candidate, and what your current status is.
2. Ask two accredited NQF 4 providers in writing: "I don't have a matric. Do you accept Grade [your highest grade],
   or RPL?" Get the fee and start date.
3. Ask the PPRA to confirm route A applies without a matric (question 1 below).
4. Decide on the adult matric by January.
5. Pay nothing before December, and nothing to any provider promising a quick FFC.

## 1. Questions for the PPRA (email or the MyPPRA portal)

1. I don't have a National Senior Certificate. Can I register as a candidate property practitioner and complete the
   FETC: Real Estate (NQF 4), or RPL against it, without one? What is my current registration status?
2. Is the competency-based PPRA Examination pathway closed to me until I have a matric, or an Amended Senior
   Certificate?
3. Once I hold a candidate FFC, which PDE sitting is the earliest I could realistically write?
4. If I charge property owners a fee for a written assessment of a rental's income, costs and options, with no mandate,
   advertising or negotiation, is that property-practitioner work? Does the answer change if the assessment recommends a
   sale or a letting?
5. Under the communique of 22 September 2026 on "powered by", may I use a trading name such as "Tidy Property Revenue
   Architect" at all while I'm registered through my agency? If so, how must it be shown?

## 2. To agree in writing with your principals

- [ ] My registration status, and the route to my own FFC, with the agency's support (employment letter, supervision).
- [ ] What I may do before, during and after candidacy.
- [ ] How mandates, advertising, offers, negotiations and transaction records are handled, and in whose name.
- [ ] Which registered firm is named to clients and on all marketing.
- [ ] The commission split, the agency's responsibilities, when I'm paid, and how disputes are resolved.
- [ ] Whether a separate assessment fee is allowed, how it is disclosed, and who invoices the client.
- [ ] Whether agency clients may be approached for the PRA service (decision 12).

### Draft message to your principals (approve and send yourself)

> Hi [name], I'd like to put our arrangement in writing so I do everything properly while I register. Could we agree,
> on one page: my current status and route to my own FFC; what I may do at each stage; how mandates, adverts and
> offers are handled and under whose name; the commission split and payment timing; and whether I may offer owners a
> separately paid written property assessment, and if so, how it's disclosed and invoiced. Happy to draft it for you to
> review. Could we find 30 minutes this week?
