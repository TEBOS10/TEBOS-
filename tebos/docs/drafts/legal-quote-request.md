**Subject:** Request for a fixed-fee quotation: SaaS client agreements, POPIA review and a client equity agreement

Dear [name / firm],

I'm the founder of TEBOS, a South African software service that builds and runs operating systems for small and
medium businesses: client portals, pipelines, billing, reporting and AI-assisted analysis, sold as a monthly
subscription. [The company is being registered with CIPC. / Registered as (company name), (registration number).]

We're preparing our first client pilot and would like a quotation, ideally fixed-fee per item, for the following.

**1. Client service agreements (two templates)**
- A standard monthly subscription agreement for small and medium businesses. Three tiers, a 6-month minimum term
  or annual payment, a monthly allowance of change requests, and pass-through running costs disclosed upfront.
- A company-tier version of the same.
- Both covering: scope and exclusions, service levels, fees and late payment, termination, limitation of liability,
  intellectual property (our core platform stays ours; the client's configuration is licensed for the term; the
  client's data stays theirs), confidentiality, and an operator agreement for the data we process on the client's
  behalf.
- Electronic acceptance (clients accept online), and whether that is sufficient.

**2. POPIA review**
- Our role as an operator for client data, and what the operator agreement must contain.
- Cross-border transfers: our database is hosted in the EU (Ireland), and AI analysis may be processed by a provider in
  the United States.
- Our own sales outreach: we plan to phone businesses we find in public listings, using business switchboard numbers
  only, and to stop permanently when asked. Please confirm this approach under section 69 and the direct-marketing
  rules, and what we must record.
- A short privacy notice for our website and client portal.

**3. Equity-for-services agreement with our first client (optional, quote separately)**
- A client will receive a defined service package in exchange for a minority equity stake ([5% / 5.1%]) in their
  business.
- We need a services agreement (the package and its limits) and an equity agreement (percentage, vesting, exit,
  dilution, transfer, buy-back and IP).

**What would help in your reply**
- The fee for each item, excluding and including VAT.
- What's included: drafts, rounds of revisions, a call.
- Turnaround time from instruction.
- Anything you need from us to start.

We aim to have items 1 and 2 in place before [date, e.g. 17 November 2026]. I'd be grateful for a reply by [date].

Kind regards,
[Name]
[Title], TEBOS
