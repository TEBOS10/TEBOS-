Dear [name],

Thank you for building BAME's operating system with us. It has been a privilege to work alongside your team, and
BAME has been the first business to show what TEBOS can do.

As TEBOS grows to serve more businesses, we are making how we work clearer and fairer for everyone. From **1 November
2026**, BAME will work with TEBOS in the following way.

**1. Requests go through TEBOS, not through me personally**
- Your team will send changes and updates to TEBOS directly, from this channel in your TEBOS account.
- Each request is recorded, so you can see what was asked, what was done and when.
- This means nothing depends on catching me on a call, and your team always knows where a request stands.

**2. A clear monthly plan**
- TEBOS now offers three plans, matched to the size of the business: Small (R500 a month), Medium (R2,500 a month)
  and Company (R7,500 a month).
- Given everything BAME's system includes, BAME sits in **Medium**. That plan includes 15 change requests each
  month, such as new photos, page updates or a changed price list.
- Requests beyond that wait for the following month, or can be covered by moving up a plan.
- Hosting and any services that run only for BAME (for example a phone number for calls) are charged at cost and
  always listed with their price before anything is set up.
- From 1 November, BAME's Medium plan is billed at **R2,500 a month** (excluding VAT), by monthly invoice, with a
  minimum term of 6 months. Paying annually instead costs 10 months' price for 12.

**3. Applying on the platform**
- So that BAME's account, plan and requests are all on record in the same way as every other client, I'd ask you to
  apply formally on the TEBOS platform: [link].
- It takes a few minutes. Your existing system and everything we've built stays exactly as it is.

**4. What we're working on for you now**
- **A media library** in your staff system, where your team keeps the website's images. A player's photo can be
  updated straight from it.
- **A specialist calling assistant** that helps close deals with prospects who've asked to hear from you. It follows
  up, answers questions from your approved information, and sends your contract or payment link when someone is ready.
  It always says it's an assistant, never changes your prices or terms, and passes anything unusual to your team.
- Before it makes a single call, we'll agree your approved offer and answers with you.

If anything here raises a question, or you'd like to talk it through, I'm glad to. Thank you again for your trust in
us. We're excited about what comes next for BAME.

Warm regards,
[your name]
