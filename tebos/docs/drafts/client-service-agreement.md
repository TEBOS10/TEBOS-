# Draft: TEBOS client service agreement and client terms

**Status: a draft for the attorney.** It is not legal advice and nobody has approved it. Nothing here is sent to a
client or published until the attorney has reviewed it and the founder has approved it (operator log, decision 3).
Once approved, it is loaded into TEBOS as a contract template. A client can then only be sent a contract made from
that approved template, and work only starts once they have accepted it. The database already enforces both rules
(`contract_templates`, `contracts` and the `opportunity` steps).

The aim, in the founder's words (1 Nov 2026): "contracts must be signed to ensure protection", and "a client [must]
know what they are getting themselves into". The agreement has to protect both sides: TEBOS from unpaid or unlimited
work, and the client from lock-in, hidden costs and losing their own things.

Square brackets mark choices for the founder or the attorney.

---

## Part A: The terms in plain words (shown to the client before they pay or sign)

1. **What you get.**
   - Your package: Small, Medium or Company.
   - A website made for your business, or changes to the one you already have.
   - Your TEBOS operating system.
   - A set number of change requests each month: 5, 15 or 40.
   - The written scope you approve before the build starts.
2. **What it costs.**
   - The monthly fee for your package: R500, R2,500 or R7,500, excluding VAT.
   - It runs for at least 6 months, or you can pay for a year up front, which is 10 months' fee for 12.
   - The fee pays for the TEBOS team's work and the TEBOS platform.
3. **Running costs.** These are things that run only for you, such as hosting, a domain, a phone number or a paid map.
   - We list each one, with its price, before we set it up. Nothing is added without your written yes.
   - [Option 1, today's model: you pay them at cost, on your own account where possible.
     Option 2, the founder's 1 Nov idea: part of your fee covers standard hosting up to R[x] a month, and anything
     above that is listed and agreed first.]
4. **What's yours.**
   - Your business data, content, photos, logo and brand.
   - Your domain and the accounts held in your name (hosting, email, payments).
   - Leaving TEBOS doesn't take any of these away. We hand over what's yours in a usable form.
5. **What's ours.**
   - The TEBOS platform, its code, its templates and its know-how.
   - You may use what we set up for you for as long as your plan is active, and for any longer period agreed at
     handover.
6. **What we promise.**
   - We start only after you've signed.
   - We work to the scope you approved, and show you the work before it goes live.
   - Nothing goes live on your site without your approval.
   - We keep your data private, under POPIA.
   - We tell you plainly when something is late or not possible.
7. **What we ask of you.**
   - Pay on time.
   - Send the content and access we need.
   - Only give us material you have the right to use.
   - Ask for changes through your TEBOS channel.
8. **Changes beyond your package.** Anything more than your monthly allowance, or outside the approved scope, is
   quoted first. It only goes ahead once you accept the quote, in writing.
9. **If a payment is missed.** We remind you. If it stays unpaid for [14] days, work pauses. Your site stays up for
   [30] days while we sort it out together, and your data is never held back from you.
10. **Ending it.**
    - After the minimum term, either side can end it with [30] days' written notice.
    - We hand over what's yours: your content, your data export, and the transfer of any accounts we hold for you.
11. **Equity partners** (no fee, ADR 0013) sign a separate equity agreement, drafted by the attorney. These terms
    apply to them too, except the fees.

## Part B: Points the full agreement must cover (for the attorney)

- **Parties and acceptance.** Electronic acceptance as TEBOS records it: the exact text, a fingerprint of it, the
  person's typed name, and the time. Is this enough under the ECT Act, and what does it need for a company signing?
- **Scope and change control.** The approved scope revision is part of the contract. Work outside it, or beyond the
  monthly change requests, needs a written quote that the client accepts.
- **Fees, VAT and late payment.** Interest on late payment [rate]. The right to pause work, never to hold back the
  client's data.
- **Running costs.** As in A3, with the option the founder chooses.
- **Service levels.** The 3-business-day delivery window for agreed requests, counted without SA public holidays
  (already enforced in TEBOS), and what happens when TEBOS misses it [credit or refund: founder decision].
- **Intellectual property.** As in A4 and A5, plus the client's warranty that the material they supply is theirs to
  use, and an indemnity for material that isn't.
- **Data protection.** TEBOS acts as the client's operator under POPIA, with an operator agreement, security
  measures, breach notification, and deletion or return of data at the end.
- **Liability.** A cap [e.g. the fees paid in the last 12 months]. No cap for fraud, gross negligence or data
  breaches caused by TEBOS [attorney to advise].
- **Third-party services.** Hosting providers, payment processors and AI providers: TEBOS chooses reputable ones and
  isn't liable for their outages beyond passing on their remedies.
- **AI-generated work** (for example, Grape Studios' image editing): the client reviews it before using it, and
  rights to the output follow the provider's terms.
- **Termination and handover.** Notice; immediate termination for material breach; the handover duties in A10.
- **Disputes.** Talk first, then mediation, then [arbitration or court]; South African law.
- **Consumer Protection Act.** Whether it applies to small clients, and the cooling-off rights that follow.
