# DRAFT: the PRA founding-client offer (stage 1, ADR 0006)

**Status: draft for the founder's approval.** Version A may be sent now; version B waits on decision 10b (ADR 0009).
- Nothing here is sent or published by TEBOS. The founder approves the text and sends it personally.
- Before the first invoice, the three checks in decision 10b are done:
  - PPRA registration;
  - VAT;
  - the client agreement.

## Who it goes to

- **Owners the founder already knows personally** who have one to fifteen rental units. A good fit is someone who has
  mentioned:
  - an empty unit;
  - late rent;
  - costs creeping up;
  - not knowing what the property really makes.
- **Not the agency's clients or contacts.** That needs the principal's permission first (decision 12), and mixing them
  could breach the founder's terms with the agency.
- **The aim is five conversations, for one yes.** The first five who say yes get the founding offer.

## Version A: discovery, send now (ADR 0009)

No price, no firm name, no offer. It asks to hear about the property, which is customer research, not marketing a
service. Record each conversation: the problem, the evidence the owner has, objections, and whether they'd pay.

> Hi [name], I'm studying how owners of rental properties decide what to do when a property isn't paying its way:
> keep and improve it, restructure, or sell. Would you be open to a 20-minute chat about your property this week?
> I'm not selling anything; I'd like to understand what you're dealing with.

## Version B: the priced founding offer, only once decision 10b is answered

Send this only after the PPRA's answer, the agency arrangement and the client agreement (`pra-compliance-checklist.md`)
are in place. The firm name and any agency name must then follow the PPRA's rules on trading names.

> Hi [name], I've started a small service for property owners: Property Revenue Architecture. Each month I look at
> what your rental actually brings in against what it costs, find where money is leaking (empty months, late rent,
> costs), fix one thing at a time, and send you a one-page update showing what was done and what changed in the
> numbers.
>
> I'm taking five founding clients at 40% off for the first six months: from R450 a month for one property (normally
> R750). In return I'd ask permission to use your results anonymously.
>
> Would you be open to a 20-minute chat this week about your property?

## What the first month delivers

These map to what TEBOS already does:
1. **Intake and evidence:**
   - the rent roll;
   - three months of statements;
   - the lease, rates and levy accounts.

   They go into the PRA case, and every figure is labelled verified, owner-reported or estimated.
2. **The baseline:** the database captures the figures as they stand, then freezes them.
3. **One fix**, chosen from the pathways and recorded as a workstream with its evidence. For example:
   - re-let a vacant unit;
   - set up rent reminders;
   - challenge a rates bill.
4. **The monthly update** (TEPOS → the case → Monthly update):
   - what was done;
   - what's next;
   - the figures then and now.

   It's printed or saved as a PDF and sent to the owner.

## What not to say

- No promised result ("you'll earn X more"). The update shows measured results only.
- No mention of software, AI or TEBOS. The owner is buying the outcome.
- No legal or tax advice, and no handling of rent money.
