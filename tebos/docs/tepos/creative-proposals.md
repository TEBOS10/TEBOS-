## 1. Portals that build themselves

**Source:** @lynam_up, "Built from the blueprint up". A website where a house builds itself as you scroll: blueprint,
foundation, framing, drywall, finished, front door.

**The idea:**
- **TEPOS city:** each building in the city goes through the same stages as its portal is built. The unbuilt portals
  already stand under scaffolding.
- **Property Revenue Architecture:** in a case, the property "builds" through assessment, plan, execution gates and
  outcome.

**Deliverable:** the TEPOS city (priority: home screen), and the Property Revenue Architecture report (priority 1).

**Rule:** a stage shows only when its work is recorded as done. No stage is drawn ahead of the facts.

**Cost:** build time only (drawn stages in the existing city). No new service or account.

**Risk:**
- low;
- heavier pages on a phone, which means keeping the drawings light.

## 2. A public-awareness globe

**Source:** OSIRIS (osiris.live) via @setupsai. A live globe of public flight, ship, satellite and event data.

**The idea:**
- **Resilience:** area-level public events (weather, disasters, disruption, market news) linked to the risks and
  playbooks in the resilience plan.
- **TEBOS:** a visual reference for the world view.

**Deliverable:** resilience step 3 (evidence-linked risks and playbooks).

**Rule:** never track people, private aircraft or individuals. The resilience dossier forbids surveillance.

**Cost:** build time. Possibly a public data source, which needs a decision before any key or account.

**Risk:**
- medium: easy to drift into tracking, so the rule above is part of the proposal;
- low value until the risk register exists.

## 3. Labelled 3D walkthroughs of a property

**Source:** @aialchemists (reposting @SimbasJungles). An AI-generated walkthrough of a beachfront apartment.

**The idea:**
- **Hospitality OS:** a walkthrough on a listing.
- **Real Estate Job portal:** virtual viewings.
- **Property Revenue Architecture:** "what this property could become".

**Deliverable:**
- Real Estate Job (priority 3);
- Property Revenue Architecture (priority 1);
- Hospitality OS (not the current focus).

**Rule:** every walkthrough is labelled as a rendering, never shown as the real property, and made only with the owner's
permission.

**Cost:** a rendering tool or service. Price unknown; it needs a decision before any spend.

**Risk:**
- medium: misrepresenting a property harms trust and may breach advertising rules, so the label is compulsory.

## 4. REEDS drafts the social calendar

**Source:** @tessa.fairbrook. Instagram connected to an AI assistant as a custom connector, making reels and a weekly
calendar with auto-publish on.

**The idea:** REEDS drafts posts and a week's calendar for TEBOS, and for clients who want it. The founder or the client
says yes or no to each.

**Deliverable:**
- TEBOS marketing (the social pack and 30-day plan already exist);
- a client service.

**Rule:**
- auto-publish stays off: nothing goes out without approval;
- no third-party connector gets account access until its operator has been checked.

**Cost:** build time, plus each platform's own access, which needs the founder's accounts and approval.

**Risk:**
- medium: account access is sensitive, so the connector must be vetted first;
- posting without approval is ruled out.

## 5. Social skills for REEDS: post, comment, reply

**Source:** @sebintel. Skills for writing posts, drafting comments and replies, improving a profile, and a "virality"
checklist (hook in the first second, pattern break at three, payoff before the loop restarts).

**The idea:** the same feature as 4, from the writing side: REEDS drafts posts, comments and replies in the house style.

**Deliverable:** as 4. REEDS's "communicates" role.

**Rule:** replies and comments are drafts until a person approves them. Never auto-reply on a client's behalf.

**Cost:** build time.

**Risk:**
- low, as drafts;
- high if anything were sent unreviewed, which is ruled out.

2. **Proposals 4 and 5 together.**
3. **Proposal 3:** when the Real Estate Job portal or Hospitality OS is the focus.
4. **Proposal 2:** after the risk register exists.
