## Ground rules (they protect the brand)

- **Nothing invented.** No fake clients, testimonials, results or numbers. TEBOS has no clients yet; say what
  TEBOS does and show how, don't claim what it achieved.
- **AI-made visuals are labelled.** Never present generated images or video of people, offices or clients as
  real.
- **No client names** without written permission. BAME is a client relationship: ask Osman before mentioning it.
- **Nothing internal.** Not the pipeline, the playbook, prices beyond the pricing page, or screenshots of real
  accounts. Show the public demo (a fictional agency), the tour, the film and the 3D board on the home page.
- **One voice:** plain, confident, specific. Short sentences. No hype words ("revolutionary", "10x", "AI-powered").

### Post 3: carousel, the chessboard model (6 slides)
1. Your business is a chessboard. You just can't see it yet.
2. **Pieces:** people, tools, providers, channels. Who owns each one?
3. **Flows:** trigger → outcome. Who really does each step?
4. **Rules:** the decisions in your head. Written down, anyone can make them.
5. **Objectives:** a number, a target, an owner, a date, read from a system.
6. See a full board: tebos-demo.vercel.app/demo

### Post 10: how an objective is measured (carousel, company page)
1. An objective isn't a wish. It's four things.
2. A number: "days a lead waits for a decision".
3. A target and a date: "at most 1 day, by 31 December".
4. An owner: one named person.
5. A source: read from a system, never typed in.
6. Once it's set, the goalposts don't move. That's how you know it worked.

- 0–3s hook: "If you took two weeks off, what would stop in your business?"

**Script C: "Before you buy another tool"** (to camera, then text on screen)
- "Before you buy another piece of software, do this: write the flow as it really happens, circle where it
  breaks, and ask whether the fix is a tool, or an owner and a written rule. Most of the time it's the second."
